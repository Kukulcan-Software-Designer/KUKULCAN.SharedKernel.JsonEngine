# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a professional, respectful and technically constructive community around **KUKULCAN.SharedKernel.JsonEngine**.

We pledge to make participation welcoming and respectful regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, sex, gender identity or expression, experience, education, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation or socioeconomic status.

## Our Standards

Expected behavior includes:

- respectful and constructive technical discussion;
- professional feedback and review;
- respect for different technical perspectives;
- responsibility for mistakes and corrections;
- protection of confidential information;
- discussion focused on the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of private information without permission, or disclosure of passwords, tokens, keys or other credentials.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, code review, documentation and other project-related communication.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers.

Security vulnerabilities must not be reported through public GitHub channels. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports fairly and take proportionate action, including warnings or restrictions on project participation when appropriate.
